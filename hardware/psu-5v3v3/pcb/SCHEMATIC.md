# Circuvent PSU-5 — 230 VAC → 5 V + 3.3 V isolated flyback

An in-house replacement for the Hi-Link HLK-PM03 module used on twelve boards,
adding a regulated 3.3 V rail so the host board can drop its own AMS1117.

**Rating:** 85–265 VAC in, 5 V @ 1.0 A and 3.3 V @ 0.6 A (5 W total, not
simultaneous maxima — the 3.3 V rail is derived from the 5 V rail).

## Why a discrete supply at all

The HLK module is potted, pre-certified and takes one footprint. Replacing it
buys three things: BOM cost (roughly ₹40 of parts against ₹120–150 for the
module), a second source that is not one vendor, and a 3.3 V rail generated
once here instead of fifteen times downstream. It costs the certification and
EMC work described at the bottom of this file, which is not a small bill.

## Topology

```
AC_N ----------+------------+            +-- HV- ---+--...   |
                                                             |

                                                              |
   T1 pin2 -- SW_DRAIN -- U1 pins 7,8 (DRAIN)                 |

   U1 pin 2 (BYPASS) --[C2 100n]-- HV-                        |

   T1 pin4/5 bias --[D6]-- VBIAS --[C4]-- HV-                 |
- - - - - - - - - - - - ISOLATION BARRIER - - - - - - - - - - + - - - - - - -
   T1 pin6 -- SEC --[D7 SS34]-- +5V --[C5 470u][C6 100u][C7 100n]-- GND
   T1 pin10 -- GND
   +5V --[R3 1k]-- PC1 LED anode ; PC1 LED cathode -- U2 cathode (TL431)
   +5V --[R4 10k]--+-- U2 REF ; +--[R5 3k3]-- GND      -> 5.0 V setpoint
   +5V --[U3 AMS1117-3.3]-- +3V3 --[C8 100u][C9 100n]-- GND
   CY1 (Y1, 2.2 nF) is the ONLY deliberate primary-to-secondary connection
   J2 = +5V / +3V3 / GND
```

## Setpoint
TL431 regulates its REF pin to 2.495 V, so

    Vout = 2.495 x (1 + R4/R5) = 2.495 x (1 + 10k/3k3) = 5.05 V

Use 1 % resistors: at 5 % the spread is ±0.25 V, which eats most of the ESP32's
supply margin once the relay coils pull the rail down.

## HV- is not ground
The primary return sits at one rail of the rectified mains — roughly 325 V
away from the secondary. It is called `HV-` everywhere in the netlist rather
than GND precisely so nobody clips a scope probe to it. **Never earth the
primary side to measure it; use an isolation transformer or a differential
probe.** The generator marks every primary net as mains, so the 8 mm barrier,
the mains netclass and the reinforced-creepage rule all apply to it.

## Pin functions asserted here, and what still needs checking
The netlist is generated, and these pin assignments are asserted in
`gen-pcb.py` (`build_netlist_psu`). Two could **not** be confirmed against a
manufacturer datasheet when this was written and are blocking pre-fab items:

| Part | Assumed pinout | Confirmed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 EE13 bobbin | 1=Np start 2=Np end · 4/5=bias · 6/10=secondary | **No** — depends on the winding house's bobbin |
| U1 TNY274PN (DIP-8B) | 1=EN/UV 2=BYPASS 4,5=SOURCE 7,8=DRAIN, 3/6 omitted | **No** |
| U2 TL431 (SOT-23) | 1=REF 2=ANODE 3=CATHODE | **No** |
| D1–D7 (SMA) | 1=cathode 2=anode | Yes — matches every other board here |
| PC1 PC817 | 1=LED anode 2=LED cathode 3=emitter 4=collector | Yes |

The TNY274 in particular has two omitted pins for creepage, so a footprint
that lands copper on positions 3 and 6 is wrong in a way that matters.

## Transformer specification (order this, do not improvise)
```
Core/bobbin   EE13 (or EF13), 10-pin vertical, 5+5, 2.54 mm pitch,
              10.16 mm row spacing  <-- CONFIRM against the supplier drawing
Lp            1.4 mH ±10 % across pins 1-2, measured at 100 kHz
Np : Ns       ~ 110 : 9   (5 V out + Schottky drop, ~65 % duty headroom)
Np : Nb       ~ 110 : 12  (bias ~ 12 V for the TNY274 BYPASS supply)
Isolation     REINFORCED, primary-to-secondary
Hipot         4000 VAC, 1 minute, primary to secondary
Construction  Triple-insulated secondary wire, or margin-wound with 3 mm
              margins each side
Leakage L     < 2 % of Lp, or the clamp dissipates more than R1/R2 can take
```
The footprint in `hardware/lib/Circuvent.pretty/Transformer_EE13_10pin.kicad_mod`
is generated by `hardware/make_lib.py`, where every dimension is a named
constant. If the supplier's bobbin differs, change the constants and re-run it.

## Layout / safety rules
- ≥ 8 mm creepage primary-to-secondary, everywhere, including under T1. A
  milled slot runs beneath the transformer and the optocoupler.
- CY1 and PC1 and T1 are the only parts that may cross the barrier.
- Bulk cap C1 negative, U1 SOURCE pins and the transformer primary return form
  the switching loop: keep it small and tight, or the board will fail
  conducted EMI and radiate into the host's ESP32.
- Snubber R1/R2 in series: a single 100 k 0805 is not rated for the 325 V+
  standing across it.
- Fuse before everything. MOV after the fuse, never before it.
- Silk: shock warning, "NO USER SERVICEABLE PARTS", mains ratings, the
  isolation barrier line, CE/BIS mark area.

## Generated-layout status — read before using the Gerbers

`gen-pcb.py psu-5v3v3 --fab` produces a correct netlist, a correct
mains/secondary split and correct design rules, and it routes most of the
board. It does **not** produce a fabricable offline SMPS, and it is not
supposed to:

- **13 clearance errors remain**, all of them `reinforced_hvdc_to_selv`
  between primary copper (HV+, CLAMP, VBIAS, BIAS) and the optocoupler
  feedback pair (FB_A, FB_K). The feedback nets have to reach PC1, which by
  definition sits on the barrier, and freerouting cannot see the 8 mm rule —
  it lives in the `.kicad_dru`, which the router never reads. It therefore
  threads the feedback pair along the barrier because that is the shortest
  path.
- **9 unconnected**, mostly on the AC input.
- Board is **77.4 x 72.0 mm** against a 70 x 55 target. Raising the target did
  not shrink it: the floor is set by the part area plus the 8 mm barrier, not
  by the packer.

**The primary of an offline flyback is hand-laid, always.** The switching loop
(C1− → U1 SOURCE → T1 primary → C1+) is the loop that determines whether this
board passes conducted EMI, and its geometry is a design decision, not a
router output. Treat the generated file as a placement and netlist starting
point that still needs a layout engineer, and re-run DRC after the hand pass.


## What this design does NOT come with
The HLK-PM03 arrives certified. This does not, and the following is real work
that has to happen before it can ship in a product:

- **EN 55032 conducted emissions.** An offline flyback is a deliberate
  325 V square-wave generator. Passing typically needs the CM choke position
  left on the board, Y-capacitor value tuning, and iterative measurement on a
  LISN. Budget several turns of the board.
- **EN 62368-1 / IS 13252 safety.** Creepage, clearance, transformer
  insulation class, fault testing (short each secondary, open the feedback
  loop, short the opto) and the hipot routine on the production line.
- **BIS registration** for sale in India — putting a mains supply inside the
  product changes the compliance scope of every board that uses it.
- **Production hipot test** on 100 % of units, 3 kVAC primary-to-secondary.

Until those are done this board is an engineering sample. The HLK module stays
the shipping choice on every board that uses it; this is the path off it, not a
drop-in swap that happens by editing a BOM line.
