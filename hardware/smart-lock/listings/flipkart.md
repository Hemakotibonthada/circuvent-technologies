## Description
The Circuvent Smart Lock Controller adds Wi-Fi lock/unlock, door sensing and auto-lock to existing 12 V electric strikes, solenoid bolts and motorized locks. A protected relay drives the lock while a reed input tracks the door. Choose fail-safe or fail-secure wiring to match local fire and egress codes. Lock hardware and 12 V supply are sold separately.
