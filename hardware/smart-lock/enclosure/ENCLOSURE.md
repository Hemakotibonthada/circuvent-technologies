# Smart Lock Controller - Enclosure spec

- **Approx size:** 70 x 45 x 25 mm. 2x M3.

- **Openings:** cable entries for 12 V in, lock output and the door reed.
- **Tooling:** injection mould (2 cavities); validate against PCB (50 x 40 mm).
