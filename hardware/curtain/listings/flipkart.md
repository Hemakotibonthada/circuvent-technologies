## Description
The Circuvent Smart Curtain & Blind Controller motorises your curtains and blinds with app, schedule and voice control. It drives an AC tubular motor through two interlocked relays, supports precise % positioning via travel-time calibration, and keeps its three wall buttons working offline. Installation by a qualified electrician; use an AC tubular motor within the current limit.
