# Smart Curtain & Blind Controller - Quick Start

## 1. Wire (electrician)
1. Switch off the mains at the board.
2. Connect incoming L/N to J1 (through the fuse).
3. Connect the motor's open lead, close lead and common to J2 (open/close/common).
4. Mount behind the curtain switch point.

## 2. Power on & connect to Wi-Fi
1. Power on - the green LED shows power.
2. Join Wi-Fi "Circuvent-Setup-XXXX" on your phone.
3. Open the setup page (or http://192.168.4.1); pick your Wi-Fi, Save & connect.

## 3. Calibrate + link
1. Open the Circuvent app -> Add a device -> enter the Device ID + Key.
2. Run Calibrate: the controller times a full open + close to learn the travel.
3. Optionally discover it in Alexa/Google as a blind.

## 4. Use it
1. Tap open/close/stop, or drag the position slider.
2. Buttons work offline (open/close/stop).
3. Schedules: open at sunrise, close at sunset, or set times.

## Troubleshooting
- Direction reversed: swap the open/close leads at J2 (or flip in the app).
- Position drifts: re-run Calibrate; ensure the motor's limit switches are set.
- Offline: hold STOP/BOOT ~5 s to reopen the Wi-Fi setup portal.
