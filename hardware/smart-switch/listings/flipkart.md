## Description
The Circuvent Smart Switch upgrades two existing switches into app-, touch- and voice-controlled loads without changing your switch plate. It installs behind the board, exposes two switches to Alexa and Google, and keeps local touch control working even when the Wi-Fi is down. Installation by a qualified electrician; observe the per-gang current limits.
