# Smart Switch (2-Gang Touch) - Quick Start

## 1. Wire (electrician)
1. Switch off the mains at the board.
2. Connect incoming L/N to J1 (through the fuse).
3. Connect each load's live to J2.OUT1/OUT2; load neutrals to common N. Observe <= 6 A/gang.
4. Tuck the module into the back-box; stick the touch pads under the plate.

## 2. Power on & connect to Wi-Fi
1. Power on - the green LED shows power.
2. On your phone, join Wi-Fi "Circuvent-Setup-XXXX".
3. The setup page opens (or visit http://192.168.4.1); pick your Wi-Fi, Save & connect.

## 3. Link account + voice
1. Open the Circuvent app -> Add a device -> enter the Device ID + Key from the sticker.
2. In Alexa/Google, discover devices - two switches appear.
3. Rename each gang (e.g., Lights, Fan).

## 4. Use it
1. Touch: tap a pad to toggle that gang (works offline).
2. Voice: "Alexa, turn on Lights".
3. Schedules: set daily on/off per gang.
4. After a power cut: gangs return to their last state.

## Troubleshooting
- Offline: hold IO0/BOOT ~5 s (or power-cycle 3x) to reopen the setup portal.
- Touch not responding: re-seat the pads flush under the plate; recalibrate in the app.
- Voice not found: re-run Alexa/Google discovery on the same 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
