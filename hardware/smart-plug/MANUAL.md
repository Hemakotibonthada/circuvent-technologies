# Smart Plug 16A (Energy Metering) - Quick Start

## 1. Plug in
1. Plug the unit into a 6/16 A wall socket rated for your load.
2. Plug your appliance into the front socket. Do not exceed 16 A.

## 2. Power on & connect to Wi-Fi
1. The green LED shows power.
2. On your phone, join Wi-Fi "Circuvent-Setup-XXXX".
3. The setup page opens (or visit http://192.168.4.1); pick your Wi-Fi, enter the password, Save & connect. The plug restarts and comes online.

## 3. Link to your account
1. Open the Circuvent app (or circuvent.com -> Store -> Devices).
2. Add a device -> enter the Device ID + Key from the sticker.
3. The plug appears with on/off, energy and schedules.

## 4. Use it
1. Button: press to toggle the load (works offline too).
2. Energy: watch live watts and daily kWh in the app.
3. Schedules: set daily on/off times.
4. After a power cut: the plug returns to its last state.

## Troubleshooting
- Offline: hold the button ~5 s to reopen the Wi-Fi setup portal.
- No power reading: give it a load; tiny standby loads read ~0 W.
- Load not switching: check the appliance is within 16 A and the fuse is intact.
