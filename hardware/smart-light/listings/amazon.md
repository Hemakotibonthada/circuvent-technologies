## Description
The Circuvent Smart Light Controller turns any 12-24 V RGBW LED strip into an app- and voice-controlled light with millions of colours, tunable white and smooth scene dimming. Four MOSFET channels drive the strip; an auxiliary relay switches a separate fixture. Match the strip voltage to your DC power supply and stay within the per-channel current.
