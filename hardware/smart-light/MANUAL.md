# Smart Light Controller (RGBW) - Quick Start

## 1. Connect the strip
1. Match your strip voltage (12 or 24 V) to a DC PSU of adequate current.
2. Wire strip +V to J2 (+V) and R/G/B/W to their terminals.
3. Connect the DC PSU to J1 (observe polarity). Optionally wire an aux lamp to J3.

## 2. Power on & connect to Wi-Fi
1. The status LED shows power.
2. On your phone, join Wi-Fi "Circuvent-Setup-XXXX".
3. Open the setup page (or http://192.168.4.1); pick your Wi-Fi, Save & connect.

## 3. Link account + voice
1. Open the Circuvent app -> Add a device -> enter the Device ID + Key.
2. Optionally discover it in Alexa/Google as a light.

## 4. Use it
1. Pick colours or white; drag brightness.
2. Tap scenes (Relax, Party, Reading) or set schedules.
3. Button: press to cycle on/off + presets (works offline).

## Troubleshooting
- Wrong colours: check the R/G/B/W wiring order at J2.
- Flicker at low brightness: use a PSU with enough headroom; keep strip runs within spec.
- Offline: hold the button ~5 s to reopen the Wi-Fi setup portal.
