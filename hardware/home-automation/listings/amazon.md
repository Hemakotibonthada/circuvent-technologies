## Description
The Circuvent Automation Hub upgrades your existing wiring into a smart,
4-channel control point for lights, fans, TV/AV and appliances. Control each
channel from the free Circuvent app or the on-device buttons, group them into
scenes, and automate them with daily schedules. Because control is local-first,
your buttons keep working even if the Wi-Fi is down. Installation by a qualified
electrician; observe the per-channel current limits.
