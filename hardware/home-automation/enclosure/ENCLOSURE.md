  white + cyan accent. DIN-rail variant optional for panels.
- **Approx size:** 130 x 90 x 40 mm (4-module footprint). 4x M3 PCB bosses.

- **Openings:** bottom cable entries for mains in + 4 load outputs (gland/knockouts).
