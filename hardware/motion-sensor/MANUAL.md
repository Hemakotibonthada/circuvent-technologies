# Wi-Fi Motion Sensor (PIR) - Quick Start

## 1. Place & power
1. Mount in a room corner, ~2 m high, facing the area to watch (away from AC vents/sunlight).
2. Power via USB-C, or fit a charged 18650 (battery variant).

## 2. Connect to Wi-Fi
1. The status LED shows power.
2. Join Wi-Fi "Circuvent-Setup-XXXX" on your phone.
3. Open the setup page (or http://192.168.4.1); pick your Wi-Fi, Save & connect.

## 3. Link to your account
1. Open the Circuvent app -> Add a device -> enter the Device ID + Key.
2. The sensor appears with motion status and an arm/disarm switch.

## 4. Use it
1. Arm it to get motion alerts; disarm when you're home.
2. Create automations: motion -> turn on a light or run a scene.
3. Battery variant: check the battery % in the app.

## Troubleshooting
- False triggers: move it away from heat/AC/sunlight; reduce the HC-SR501 sensitivity pot.
- Misses motion: increase sensitivity; lower the mount slightly; face the walk-path.
- Short battery life: raise the re-trigger hold time; disable the optional relay.
