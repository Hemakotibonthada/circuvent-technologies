# Agri GSM + Wi-Fi Pump Starter - Quick Start

## 1. Wire (electrician)
1. Switch off supply at the panel.
2. Power the unit from one phase L/N at J1 (through the fuse).
3. Wire J2 to the contactor coil A1/A2 (in series with your existing start circuit).
4. Wire each phase to a J3 sense input. Mount the IP54 box; use glands.

## 2. Insert SIM & power on
1. Insert an activated 2G SIM (SMS enabled).
2. Power on - the power LED lights; the GSM module registers in ~30 s.

## 3. Link + Wi-Fi (optional, for OTA)
1. Open the Circuvent app -> Add a device -> enter the Device ID + Key.
2. Optionally connect Wi-Fi via the "Circuvent-Setup-XXXX" portal for updates.

## 4. Use it
1. Give a missed call to the unit's number to toggle the pump, or send START/STOP by SMS.
2. Or tap on/off in the app.
3. If supply drops, the pump stops safely and restarts when power returns.

## Troubleshooting
- Pump won't start: check the phase-sense inputs read power present and the contactor coil is wired to J2.
- No GSM control: verify SIM balance + 2G coverage; keep the antenna outside the metal panel.
- Frequent stops: confirm stable supply on the sensed phase(s); check the overload relay.
