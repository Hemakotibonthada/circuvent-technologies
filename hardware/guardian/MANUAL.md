# Guardian GPS + GSM SOS Beacon - Quick Start

## 1. Insert SIM & charge
1. Insert an activated 2G SIM (with SMS + a small balance).
2. Charge fully via USB-C - the charge LED turns off when done.

## 2. Set your trusted contact
1. Open the Circuvent app -> Add a device -> enter the Device ID + Key.
2. Enter the trusted phone number(s) that will receive the SOS SMS + call.
3. Connect the beacon to Wi-Fi once (for OTA) via the setup portal "Circuvent-Setup-XXXX".

## 3. Test it
1. Go outdoors for a GPS fix (first fix can take a minute).
2. Press and hold SOS - confirm the SMS with a map link and the call arrive.
3. Check location + battery appear in the app.

## 4. Use it
1. Carry it on the lanyard; keep it charged.
2. Press SOS in an emergency; the buzzer confirms.
3. Disarm in the app to silence accidental presses; re-arm afterwards.

## Troubleshooting
- No SMS/call: check the SIM balance, 2G coverage and that the trusted number is saved.
- No GPS fix: test outdoors with a clear sky view; keep the GPS antenna up.
- Short runtime: charge fully; a fresh protected 18650 gives the best life.
