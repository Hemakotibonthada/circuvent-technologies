# Wi-Fi Energy Monitor (Clamp CT) - Quick Start

## 1. Fit the CT clamp (electrician)
1. Switch off supply at the meter/DB box.
2. Clip the CT around a SINGLE insulated live conductor (not the whole cable).
3. Plug the CT into the 3.5 mm jack. Restore supply.

## 2. Power on & connect to Wi-Fi
1. Power the monitor (mains terminals or USB-C).
2. Join Wi-Fi "Circuvent-Setup-XXXX" on your phone.
3. Open the setup page (or http://192.168.4.1); pick your Wi-Fi, Save & connect.

## 3. Link + calibrate
1. Open the Circuvent app -> Add a device -> enter the Device ID + Key.
2. Run a quick calibration against a known load (e.g., a 1000 W heater) to set CT_CAL.

## 4. Use it
1. Watch live watts on the dashboard.
2. Track daily/weekly kWh and estimated cost.
3. Set a high-usage alert threshold.

## Troubleshooting
- Reads zero: check the CT is fully clamped around a single live wire and plugged in.
- Reading is off: re-run calibration; confirm the CT range matches your load.
- Offline: hold BOOT ~5 s to reopen the Wi-Fi setup portal.
