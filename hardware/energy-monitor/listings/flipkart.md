## Description
The Circuvent Wi-Fi Energy Monitor shows exactly how much power your home is using and what it costs. Clip the included CT clamp around your incoming live conductor and read live watts, cumulative kWh and cost in the app - no rewiring. Add the optional voltage-sense module for true power and power factor. A qualified electrician should open the meter/DB box to fit the clamp.
