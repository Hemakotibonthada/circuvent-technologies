## Description
Circuvent AquaGuard turns any ordinary water pump into a smart, self-managing
system. Fit the waterproof ultrasonic probe on your tank, wire the controller to
your motor's contactor, and AquaGuard does the rest - filling the tank on a
schedule you control and shutting the pump off the instant the tank is full or
if the pump runs dry. Get live level on your phone, set start/stop levels, and
relax. Installation by a qualified electrician via a contactor (up to ~1.5 HP
single-phase).
