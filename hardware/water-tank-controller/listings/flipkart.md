## Description
Make your water pump smart. AquaGuard automatically fills your tank and stops the
motor when it's full - or instantly if the pump runs dry - protecting your motor
and preventing overflow and wastage. Monitor live tank level and control the pump
from the free Circuvent app. Installs on any single-phase pump via a contactor
(qualified electrician recommended).
