Circuvent is the companion app for Circuvent smart home hardware. Set your devices up over Wi-Fi, control them from anywhere, and see what your home is actually doing.

CONTROL EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
Lights, fans, plugs, switchboards, curtains, locks, water pumps, gates, doors, cameras and safety sensors all live on one screen. Group them by room, or pin the ones you use most to the top.

SET UP WITHOUT A MANUAL
New hardware joins your Wi-Fi from inside the app. Scan the QR label, pick your network, done. Moved house or changed router? Push new Wi-Fi credentials to a device without factory-resetting it.

SCENES AND SCHEDULES
Build a scene once — "Good night", "Leaving" — and run it with one tap. Schedule any switch by time and day, with per-channel control on multi-gang boards. Automations react to what your devices report: tank level, motion, temperature, gas.

WATER AND PUMPS
AquaGuard and WaterTank Duo show live tank levels, run the pump automatically between thresholds you set, and cut it if it ever runs dry.

SAFETY THAT ACTS
The Sentinel panel watches for combustible gas and smoke. On detection it does more than beep: it cuts the appliances you nominate and starts an exhaust fan. Guardian and motion sensors report intrusion and SOS.

ENERGY YOU CAN READ
See live wattage per device, daily and monthly totals, and which appliances are actually costing you money. Metered plugs and switchboards report voltage, current, power factor and units.

LIVE VIDEO
View Circuvent cameras and video doorbells in the app, take a snapshot, and get told when they see motion.

WORKS WHEN THE INTERNET DOESN'T
Touch panels and wall switches keep working with no network at all — the relay is local. When the connection comes back the app catches up. A tap on a physical panel shows up in the app straight away, not on the next refresh.

BUILT FOR REAL HOMES
· Light and dark themes, and a glass theme if you like it
· Rename any channel — "Gang 2" becomes "Porch light"
· Kiosk mode for a wall-mounted tablet
· Weather, with advice tied to your devices
· Voice commands processed on the device, no cloud transcription
· Full timeline of what changed, when, and what changed it

ABOUT PERMISSIONS
Location and nearby-devices permissions are used only to find and join your device's temporary setup hotspot during onboarding. Android requires them for any app that scans or connects to Wi-Fi networks. Your location is never collected, stored or shared. The camera is used to scan the QR label on your hardware.

You need Circuvent hardware and an account to use this app.
