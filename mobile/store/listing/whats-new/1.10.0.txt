New in 1.10.0

· Sentinel safety panel support — gas and smoke detection, temperature and
  humidity, and relays that cut appliances automatically when gas is found.
· Swipe from the left edge to go back, anywhere in the app.
· Screens no longer say "nothing here" while they are still loading.
· Bigger, properly labelled back and header buttons, so they are easier to hit
  and work correctly with screen readers.
· Faster, steadier device status updates.
